## You can, without asking

- Refer to the project by its name, accurately: "works with Relevo", "built with Relevo", "a fork of Relevo".
- Use the logo, unmodified, to identify or link to this project, for example in the README or docs of a project that uses or integrates Relevo, in an article, a talk or a slide.

When you use the logo, follow the [usage guide](docs/brand/guia-de-uso.html): keep its proportions and colors, leave the clear space around it, and do not mix its versions at the same size.
